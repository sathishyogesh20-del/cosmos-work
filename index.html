About this build

Cosmos Work bundles everything into one installable app: tasks, a focus timer, a calendar, a QR scanner, live weather, a calculator and an AI assistant — all running entirely in your browser with no backend server. Use the sidebar (or the tab bar on mobile) to jump between stations.
